associate. I told him no and I told him it was about board size. That is true and it
is not the reason.

room where we set our own price.

Sequoia gives Marcus two voices at a four-director table before the raise. Elena
would notice within a meeting. Tom would notice immediately, and Tom is the one
director I cannot afford to have thinking the process is being managed.

So: declined, on board size, and that is what the record will say. Whitfield's Corvid
seat does not go in the minutes, does not go in the comp committee note, and does not
go to Priya. She would be right about it and she would say so in front of Marcus.

If this ever has to be defended, the defensible version is the one I gave him.
