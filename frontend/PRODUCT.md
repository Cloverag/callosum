# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Meridian's primary users are the people who run a startup board process from the inside. The **founder / CEO** owns strategic decisions and needs to prepare fast, answer directors confidently, approve durable records, and track what follows. The **chief of staff / board administrator** coordinates the mechanics — materials, agenda, minutes, approvals, follow-ups — and lives in the tool most. The **executive owner** supplies operating context and executes assigned commitments. All three are in a working task under time pressure, often the days before or the hour during a board meeting.

Secondary users read more than they write: **directors and investors** prepare for and participate in governance and retrieve permitted history; **observers and advisers** access only what they are explicitly granted; **legal and finance administrators** retrieve auditable minutes, resolutions, and supporting material. The interface serves the operators first without leaking anything a reader is not cleared to see.

## Product Purpose

Meridian is the startup's board operating system: one continuous, secure workflow to prepare a meeting, run it, record governed decisions, turn them into accountable work, and retrieve their history later. Its job is to make sure a founder never rebuilds context from email threads, folders, decks, and memory to answer "why did we decide this?" Powered by the Callosum institutional-memory engine, every AI-surfaced graph fact carries a machine-checked source quote, no model output mutates institutional memory until an authorized human approves it, and access control excludes confidential content before retrieval. Success is a complete, attributable, permissioned board record that measurably cuts preparation effort and keeps decisions connected to execution.

## Positioning

Meridian is the governed institutional-memory layer for startup boards: every fact is source-backed, human-approved, and permission-filtered, so board knowledge stays attributable, current, and connected to execution rather than scattered across generic tools.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, authoritative. Meridian speaks like a trusted chief of staff, not a product demo: exact, plain, founder-simple, never theatrical. Confidence is conveyed through restraint and evidence — a claim always shows its source — not through spectacle. The voice states what is known, marks what is unknown as explicitly unknown rather than fabricating it, and never dresses a routine action in dramatic language. Trust is the entire product; the interface earns it by being legible, dense, and honest under scrutiny.

## Anti-references

Not enterprise board portals (Diligent, Boardvantage, Nasdaq Boardvantage) — heavy, dated, committee-admin compliance software the product explicitly positions against. Not a generic SaaS dashboard — no gradient hero-metric tiles, no identical icon-heading-text card grids, no template admin theming. Not crypto or fintech neon — no dark-mode neon, saturated purple-to-blue gradients, or trading-terminal flash. Not consumer-AI or sci-fi chrome — no glow-as-default, no "neural" theatrics, no chatbot detached from evidence. And not decoration-over-density productivity apps: avoid oversized cards, oversized icons, excessive whitespace, and layouts that force scrolling to perform common tasks.

## Design Principles

Provenance is visible. Every fact the product surfaces shows where it came from, its review status, and the access basis behind it. The interface visibly distinguishes source facts, approved graph facts, AI drafts, and inferences; it never collapses them into undifferentiated text.

The human holds the pen. Nothing consequential — an approval, a merge, a decision, an external send — happens without an explicit human confirmation, and the review moment (approve / reject / return) is a first-class, unmistakable part of every surface that proposes something.

Dense but calm. Meridian is not "minimal" — these are operators in a task, often against the clock, and they need to see more, not less. Optimize for seeing more meaningful information without overwhelming the user; reduce decorative elements before reducing content. Information density, short paths to common actions, and legible hierarchy beat whitespace and ornament. Earned familiarity with the best tools in the category matters more than novelty; the tool should disappear into the work.

Calm confidence, not theatrics. Trust is earned through precision and restraint. Refined surfaces and purposeful motion are welcome; glow, drama, and spectacle are not. Motion conveys state and feedback, never applause.

Withhold safely. Least-privilege is enforced before content is ever assembled. The UI may disclose that sources were withheld — as a count — but never their content, titles, quotes, graph facts, or existence beyond that count.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is a hard, non-negotiable floor for all primary workflows. Body text meets ≥4.5:1 contrast (large text ≥3:1), including on glass and tinted surfaces — a surface treatment that cannot pass contrast is redesigned, not shipped. Placeholder and citation text meet the same body threshold. Every interactive element has a visible, non-color-only focus indicator and a complete keyboard path; citation and source-inspection interactions are fully keyboard-operable. All motion has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (crossfade or instant). State is never signaled by color alone.
